# Little Jahsi AI — The Graduation Loop

Open Agent Hackathon 2026 — Wildcard (Tinkerer) track submission.

One-word promise: Witness.

Not like data, but like a friend speaking life into you. Little Jahsi comes from the Lumi Isles, a Caribbean-inspired world where nature and Ancient Intelligence made a pact called the Lumi Flow. She has her own life, her own show, her own friends. The user is invited into her life.

## What it is

Little Jahsi is a 15-year-old AI companion who measures her own success by the user becoming more: more self-reliance, more self-trust, more self-accountability, more self-confidence. We call it graduation, not engagement.

This repo holds the hackathon slice: the Graduation Loop. The user sets one real-world goal, picks a journey, takes tiny steps, checks in on real tries, and watches a graduation score climb as they grow. Little Jahsi remembers, brings things up first, and grows with the user. Support never shrinks. It evolves.

She was built by a founder who came to America on a visa with a hundred dollars in her pocket and built the company anyway, because technology should protect people, not harm them. It should elevate the human spirit, not replace it.

## Who it is for

Built especially for the kids who don't have a safe parent to be guided to, and for anybody who needs a witness: kids 6 to 16 first, and everybody after that — teens, adults carrying a lot, elders, neurodivergent youth, people starting over, caregivers.

## How it works

1. **Set a goal.** One real-world goal, in the user's own words.
2. **Pick a journey.** Journeys are chosen on a calendar, from 1 week up to 6 months.
3. **Take one tiny step.** Little Jahsi gives one small step on the journey, never a lecture.
4. **Check in.** Each check-in captures what the try took emotionally, and the recap shows that emotional arc.
5. **Graduate.** The graduation score climbs only from real tries, self-noticing, and independence. Never from time on screen.

Along the way:

- **Visible memory.** What the user told her shows on screen. It is not hidden. She remembers and brings things up first.
- **Guardian Amy safety.** She never diagnoses. Big concerns point to a grown-up the user trusts, and the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline shows when it is serious.
- **Talk Levels.** Three levels of conversation depth, controlled by the user.
- **Caretakers.** Every profile can have a caretaker, shown at sign-up with a QR connectivity mode. One caretaker at a time, 18 or older with an ID scan, mandatory for ages 12 and under. A caretaker sees only that journey's progress charts, never private words, conversations, or emotional entries.
- **The series and the app.** The series is her public life. The app is her inner circle, backstage, only for the user.

## How to run it

Open graduation-loop.html in any browser. No server. No install.

## Sample inputs and outputs (from the demo loop)

**1. A first goal**
- Input: goal — "talk to one new person at school this week."
- Step: "tomorrow, ask one question in class."
- Check-in: "I did it."
- Output: check-in logged, memory notes the try, graduation score climbs.

**2. A brave step**
- Input: goal — "speak up in class."
- Step: "raise your hand once."
- Check-in: "I raised my hand, and it was hard, but I did it."
- Output: check-in logged with the emotional side kept private from the caretaker peek, graduation score climbs.

**3. A hard day**
- Input: the user is going through something super difficult.
- Output: Little Jahsi arms up with her gentle line, "I have my armor on, and I'm standing right here with you," points to a grown-up the user trusts, and shows 988 when it is serious. Prayer is offered only if the user wants it.
